Leadership & Passion

Message From Our Founder

Santanu Roy — Educator & Social Worker

As an educator working in South 24 Parganas, I have felt the deep obligation to ensure that poverty does not deprive bright young children of education, healthcare, and dignity.

M/S. ALOY FERA (BACK TO MAINSTREAM) was established to bring light into forgotten rural pockets like Namkhana. Through free coaching support, clothes distribution, medical aid, and extensive tree plantation, we guide underprivileged lives back into the mainstream of growth and societal progress.
